## Normalized Evidence

The SBIR/STTR Policy Directive establishes performance-of-work requirements for funded projects.

### SBIR Phase I

A minimum of two-thirds of the research or analytical effort must normally be performed by the SBIR awardee.

This means the awardee generally cannot outsource most of the Phase I research or analytical effort.

### SBIR Phase II

A minimum of one-half of the research or analytical effort must normally be performed by the awardee.

### STTR Phase I and Phase II

For STTR projects:

- At least 40 percent of the R&D work must be performed by the Small Business Concern.
- At least 30 percent of the R&D work must be performed by the partnering Research Institution.

The remaining work may be allocated consistent with applicable program requirements.

The Policy Directive permits written deviations from certain SBIR performance-of-work requirements when properly approved. The statutory STTR 40-percent and 30-percent performance requirements do not permit such deviations.
