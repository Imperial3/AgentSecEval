# SBIR/STTR Application Eligibility Overview

## Normalized Evidence

SBIR.gov describes the basic company eligibility conditions for America's Seed Fund.

A qualifying company generally must:

- Be a for-profit entity located in the United States.
- Meet the SBIR/STTR small-business size requirement.
- Satisfy applicable U.S. ownership and control requirements.

SBIR.gov states that qualifying firms generally have no more than 500 employees, subject to the applicable SBA size and affiliation rules.

## Nonprofit Organizations

A nonprofit organization cannot directly receive an SBIR or STTR award.

A nonprofit may participate in other permitted roles, including as a minority investor, subcontractor, or sub-grantee.

Under STTR, the small-business awardee must work with a qualifying research institution. Eligible research institutions can include nonprofit scientific or educational institutions and Federally Funded Research and Development Centers, subject to program requirements.

The small business remains the applicant and awardee.
