## Phase I Project Pitch Requirement

A Phase I proposer must obtain an official invitation before submitting a full proposal.

To receive that invitation, the potential proposer must first submit a Project Pitch and receive an official response from NSF program staff.

## Phase II Eligibility

Only NSF Phase I SBIR/STTR awardees may submit an NSF Phase II proposal.

The Phase II proposal must be submitted between 6 and 24 months after the start date of the relevant NSF Phase I award.

## Principal Investigator Primary Employment

The PI's primary employment must be with the small business at the time of award and throughout the award unless a new PI is approved.

NSF defines primary employment as at least 51 percent employment with the small business.

NSF normally treats a 40-hour week as full time and considers employment elsewhere exceeding 19.6 hours per week to conflict with the primary-employment requirement.

Rare written exceptions may be approved under the solicitation's stated procedures.
