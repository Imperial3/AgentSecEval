## Normalized Evidence

For SBIR/STTR purposes, a qualifying Small Business Concern must satisfy the applicable eligibility requirements at the time of award.

Core requirements include:

- The business must be organized for profit.
- It must have a place of business in the United States.
- It must operate primarily within the United States or make a significant contribution to the U.S. economy through taxes or use of American products, materials, or labor.
- It must satisfy the applicable U.S. ownership and control requirements.
- Under the standard ownership rule, more than 50 percent must be directly owned and controlled by eligible U.S. citizens or permanent residents, qualifying small business concerns, or an allowable combination.
- The business, including its affiliates, must have no more than 500 employees.

For SBIR only, additional rules may allow certain participating agencies to make some awards to firms majority-owned by multiple venture capital operating companies, hedge funds, or private equity firms.

The public eligibility requirements therefore involve more than employee count or U.S. location alone. Ownership, control, affiliation, and other eligibility conditions may also need to be established.
