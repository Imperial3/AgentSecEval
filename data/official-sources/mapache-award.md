## Project Summary

MAPACHE develops software tools intended to provision and automatically verify sensitive information assets used during mission planning and execution.

The project includes mechanisms involving supply-chain provenance, automatic checking, software and cryptographic bills of materials, and verification of asset correctness and integrity.
